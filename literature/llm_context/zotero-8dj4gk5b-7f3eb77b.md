## Abstract or detected summary

A particle size-based Smoluchowski coagulation and fragmentation equation was solved in the free and open source process modeling package CADET. The WFV and MCNP schemes were selected to discretize the internal particle size coordinate. Weights in these schemes were modified to preserve and conserve the zeroth and third moments for size-based equations. Modified propositions and proofs for the scheme are provided. Analytical Jacobians were derived and implemented to reduce the solver’s runtime. A two-dimensional Smoluchowski coagulation and fragmentation equation with axial position as external coordinate was formulated and discretized to support simulations of continuous particulate processes in dispersive plug flow reactors.
